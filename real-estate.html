How project outputs work

  1. Pick a property first; the review moves to that property's media.
  2. Click photos or videos to select the ones to use.
  3. Go to Titles and change only the captions that should be different in the final product.
  4. Go to Order and drag the selected media into the PDF or video sequence.
  5. Go to Output, choose PDF, video, or both, then view or download the deliverables.
  6. View files on mobile or download them on a computer; capture or share whatever you are seeing in the way that works best for you.
  7. Load a previous selection file if you want to resume from an older batch.
