# Photos By Elie Game Plan

Last updated: 2026-07-13

## First Commercial Target

Generate EUR 1,000 in the first month by selling two EUR 500 Real Estate
Property Launch packages.

The initial service area is the Marbella-Malaga-Nerja corridor, starting from
Alhaurin de la Torre. English-facing agencies and independent agents are the
most practical first prospects while Elie's Spanish develops.

## Current Commercial Decisions

- The sub-dollar storefront prices were rehearsal prices, not the intended
  commercial position.
- AI-generated images will be retired from the commercial storefront.
- The two Corine properties may be used as anonymous portfolio proof.
- The Corine work should demonstrate delivery experience, but a stronger,
  controlled property shoot should eventually become the hero case study.
- Product development is secondary during this launch unless it directly helps
  win or deliver paid work.

## The Offer

### Property Launch Pilot - EUR 500

- 25 edited property photographs
- One 45-60 second vertical reel
- Private client gallery
- Simple property presentation page
- Delivery within 72 hours
- One revision round
- 50% deposit to confirm the booking

Two paid packages reach the first-month revenue target.

## Division Of Work

### Codex Owns The Production Desk

- Finish and organize the portfolio material.
- Prepare case studies, sales collateral, proposals, and shoot briefs.
- Research and rank suitable prospects.
- Draft personalized outreach and follow-up messages.
- Prepare call scripts and objection responses.
- Maintain the pipeline and convert replies into clear next actions.
- Review results and improve the offer based on market evidence.

### Elie Owns The Human Edge

- Make the final visual and brand judgments.
- Use personal and family relationships to find warm introductions.
- Send outreach personally and conduct calls.
- Build trust with agents, owners, and property managers.
- Visit and photograph properties.
- Close bookings and collect deposits.
- Give Codex concise feedback from every real conversation.

Codex should make sure Elie never starts a sales or delivery task from a blank
page. Elie remains the recognizable photographer and decision-maker.

## Launch Week

### Monday - Build The Proof

Codex finishes a cohesive 12-image Corine portfolio, creates two short anonymous
case studies, and prepares the EUR 500 offer presentation.

Elie chooses the photographs that honestly represent his taste and flags edits
that feel inaccurate, artificial, or overworked.

**Outcome:** Credible work that can be shown without explanation or apology.

### Tuesday - Build The Market

Codex researches 30 suitable agencies and independent agents:

- 15 in Malaga-Mijas
- 10 in Marbella
- 5 in Nerja

Each prospect should have a reason for inclusion, useful contact information,
likely working language, and an estimate of property tier.

Elie identifies warm connections and approves one English and one simple-Spanish
outreach message.

**Outcome:** A ranked prospect list rather than a generic directory dump.

### Wednesday - Go To Market

Codex prepares five personalized messages, a short call script, and concise
answers to likely objections.

Elie sends the five messages personally and makes two calls. The goal is to
begin useful conversations, not force an immediate online purchase.

**Outcome:** Five real approaches and the first market feedback.

### Thursday - Follow Up And Sell

Codex turns responses into next actions, tailors the presentation for interested
agents, and prepares quotes or shoot briefs.

Elie continues the conversations, offers short introductory calls, and tries to
book either a paid shoot or a high-quality portfolio session.

**Outcome:** Appointments, qualified leads, and clear objections to address.

### Friday - Close The Loop

Codex follows up with Wednesday's nonresponders, updates the pipeline, identifies
what generated interest, and prepares the next prospect batch.

Elie reviews which conversations felt natural, which objections repeated, and
whether the price and package felt credible.

**Outcome:** A measurable pipeline and evidence for improving week two.

## Daily Rhythm

On a normal non-shooting day, Elie's commitment is approximately 60-90 minutes:

1. **15 minutes:** Review the prepared actions for the day.
2. **30 minutes:** Send five personalized approaches.
3. **15-30 minutes:** Reply, call, and follow up.
4. **10 minutes:** Report outcomes to Codex in plain language.

Codex works around that human window by preparing the next actions, maintaining
the pipeline, improving collateral, and processing incoming opportunities.

On shooting days, sales activity drops to essential replies. The property gets
Elie's full attention. The following day becomes the editing, delivery, and
client follow-up day.

| Monday | Pipeline review, prospect research, and scheduling |

## Weekly Scorecard

Track outcomes, not activity for its own sake:

- 25 personalized approaches sent
- 10 calls attempted
- Replies received
- Qualified conversations started
- Introductory calls or meetings booked
- Quotes sent
- Deposits collected
- Shoots completed
- Revenue invoiced and revenue received
- Delivery time and client feedback

## Working Rules

1. Finish the 12-image Corine proof set, then stop polishing and begin selling.
2. Do not wait for a perfect website before contacting prospects.
3. Personalize every first approach; five strong messages are better than fifty
   generic ones.
4. Treat repeated objections as product information and revise the offer weekly.
5. Protect shooting and delivery quality once a client says yes.
6. Build new software only when it removes a demonstrated sales or delivery
   blocker.
7. Ask for permission to use successful paid work as a named case study and ask
   for a testimonial after delivery.
