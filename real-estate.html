How project outputs work

  1. Pick one or more shoots first; the Photos step shows only those media.
  2. Click photos or videos to select the ones to use.
  3. Go to Titles and change only the captions that should be different in the final product.
  4. Go to Order and drag the selected media into the PDF or video sequence.
  5. Go to Output to preview the PDF or video, then download the PDF or video file you need.
  6. Open or download the generated PDF and video files on phone or desktop; save or share them in the way that works best for you.
  7. Open a saved selection from the shelf when you want to resume or revise older work.
