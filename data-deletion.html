Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Photos By Elie is a personal photography site operated by Elie Cohen. If you used a Photos By Elie app or contacted Photos By Elie and want associated personal data deleted, send a request to ec92009@gmail.com.

How To Request Deletion

Email ec92009@gmail.com with the subject line "Photos By Elie data deletion request". Include the name, email address, social account, order reference, or other identifier you used with Photos By Elie so the relevant data can be located.

After the request is received, Photos By Elie will delete or anonymize data that is no longer needed for security, legal, fraud-prevention, dispute, or transaction-record purposes.

Facebook And Instagram App Data

If you authorized a Photos By Elie Meta app, you may also remove the app from your Facebook or Instagram account settings. To request deletion of any Photos By Elie records associated with that authorization, use the email process above.
